Patterns are located `src/patterns`, they are split into 6 types.

## Atoms

These are the building blocks of a project.

## Partials

These are global patterns shared to components, they contain Atoms.

## Components

The are a combination of Atoms and Partials.

## Containers

These are wrapper elements, you can pass any type into these.

## Layouts

These are full layouts consisting of Atoms, Partials and Components.

## Previews

These are for displaying the above.
